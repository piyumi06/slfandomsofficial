💛 Jasper

JASP.ER is a four-member Thai T-Pop boy group formed under RISER MUSIC,
an artist label subsidiary of GMMTV, consisting of members Joong, Aou, Santa, and Pond.
Officially debuting as a project group before transitioning into a permanent unit,
their group name acts as an acronym for the members' initials while drawing inspiration from the jasper gemstone.
