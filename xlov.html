🖤 XLOV

XLOV (엑스러브) is a four-member South Korean group under WM Entertainment.
The group consists of Wumuti, Rui, Hyun, and Haru.
They debuted on January 7, 2026, with "I'mma Be" and a brand-new concept dedicated to being genderless or gender-free,
marking a fresh and alluring point of view within K-pop.
They are currently experiencing a growing fanbase through their passion, self-made work, and dedication.
